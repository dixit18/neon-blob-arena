# MARKETS — where the world plays, and what we build for them. Sources: Circana,
Sensor Tower, Newzoo, SteamDB, MIXI/Naavik, Lumikai, GameTeahouse, WN Hub, TAdviser
(all Sept 2026 sweep). Rule 10 applies: Nova finds, Cross counters, ledger decides.

## USA — $68.9B (2026). Habit-locked top 5, unchanged 2 years.
- Plays: Fortnite (650M reg, 1-in-3 console users weekly), Roblox (380M MAU, 5M+ concurrent),
  Minecraft (350M sold), CoD, GTA V. CS2/Valorant for intensity, Candy Crush for casual.
- Reads: top-5 static since 2022 = players bored but sticky. Discovery via TikTok clips
  (2-second-readable gameplay wins). Low commitment beats depth for 10 spare minutes.
- Build for USA: knockout party formats (Fall Guys-shaped hole on browser), clip-first
  mechanics, skill-ceiling duels. Avoid: BR-shooter (Fortnite/Krunker own it).

## India — 600M gamers, $1.5B → $2.4B (2029). Volume + culture.
- Plays: Free Fire MAX + BGMI (~300M MAU combined, 94% of shooter revenue), Ludo King
  (~1B installs), Cricket League, Carrom Pool, Subway Surfers. 2GB-RAM devices rule;
  bite-sized ad-monetized sessions win. RMG ban → skill/strategy surge (4X +77%).
- Reads: cultural fit beats production value (Ludo/Carrom/Cricket out-download global
  franchises). Localize or lose. Low-end performance is THE feature.
- Build for India: LUDO CLASH (board, turn-based), CRICKET SMASH (timing batting),
  RUMBLE RACE (short knockout). All mobile-first, DPR-capped, Hindi-ready strings.

## China — 722M gamers. MOBA + live-ops mastery (not directly servable, learnable).
- Plays: Honor of Kings (139M China DAU, $14.5B lifetime), Game for Peace, Genshin.
- Reads (steal, don't chase): 5–12 min matches, event calendar = installs, social-graph
  invites, seasonal resets, young/mobile-first. Regulatory cage (ISBN, minor caps)
  doesn't apply to browser — our opening is frictionless play with live-ops rhythm.
- Build lesson: seasons + events + shareable clips in EVERY game (already our loop).

## Russia/CIS — $2.3B, 106–110M audience. Tanks + web portals.
- Plays: World of Tanks (450K concurrent record — beat CS2+Dota locally), CS2 (16% of
  global base is Russian), Dota 2. Web via Yandex/VK/OK: arcades, puzzles, idle, farms
  growing 20–30%/yr. PC mostly, mobile rising.
- Reads: military-vehicle fantasy + competitive fairness + browser convenience.
  Portal distribution (Yandex Games model) validates our marketplace shape.
- Build for RU: STEEL SWARM (tank arena) is the single highest-fit concept on this page.

## Europe — CS2 heartland (Germany/Poland), football everywhere, Roblox/Minecraft youth.
- Reads: same as RU/US — tanks for the east, football for the south/west, party for kids.
- Build for EU: STEEL SWARM covers east; RUMBLE RACE covers party; football waits.

## SEA / Brazil / MENA / Japan — Free Fire + MLBB belt, Honor of Kings expanding.
- Reads: short matches, squad social, low-end devices — identical constraints to India.
  Winning India = winning these for free.
